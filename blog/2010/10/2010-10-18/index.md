==========

Yes, "pirate" comes from the Greek noun PEIRATES (make the second -e-

long), a deverbative of PEIRAO (make that -o long) meaning "attempt",

"endeavour" or "try". Not far from the spirit of the true creative

soul (but, mind you, those ancient pirates were dreadful folks.)

from Daniel Riaño

============

Etymology

The English "pirate" is derived from the Latin term pirata and that from Greek "πειρατής" (peiratēs), "brigand",[1] in turn from "πειράομαι" (peiráomai), "attempt", from "πεῖρα" (peîra), "attempt, experience".[2] The word is also cognate to peril.[3]

<http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piracy#Etymology>
